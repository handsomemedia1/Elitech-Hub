What You Will Learn

This isn't a passive course. Every session builds on the last. By the end, you will have a working security automation project and real internship experience.

WEEK 1: Foundation (Environment, Linux & PowerShell)

Day 1: Setting Up Your Security Lab

Install and configure Ubuntu in a virtual machine. Understand the cybersecurity lab environment. Navigation, file system, users and permissions.

Day 2: Linux for Security Professionals

Essential CLI commands every security person must know. Network configuration, process monitoring, log reading, and service management on Ubuntu.

Day 3: PowerShell for Defenders

Why PowerShell is the language of Windows security. Write scripts to audit systems, pull event logs, and detect anomalies automatically.

Day 4: PowerShell for Attackers

Understanding how attackers use PowerShell — so you can defend against it. Red team thinking for blue team advantage. Lab exercise.

WEEK 2: Threat Landscape + AI as Your Weapon

Day 5: How Attacks Actually Happen

Reconnaissance, exploitation, persistence, exfiltration. The attacker's full playbook — explained practically so you know what to protect.

Day 6: AI for Threat Detection

Using AI models to analyse network logs, detect anomalies, and flag suspicious behaviour. Hands on with real log datasets.

Day 7: Security Automation with Python + AI APIs

Write your first AI powered security script. Automate repetitive analyst tasks. Connect to AI APIs and build a simple alert system.

Day 8: SIEM Basics + AI Enhanced Monitoring

What is a SIEM and why it matters. Set up basic monitoring. Layer AI on top of it to reduce alert fatigue. Lab session.

WEEK 3: Securing AI (Protecting the Systems That Protect You)

Day 9: How AI Systems Get Attacked

Prompt injection, adversarial inputs, model poisoning, data extraction. The threats you don't hear about — explained clearly.

Day 10: Secure AI Development Principles

Building AI powered tools that are safe, responsible, and hard to exploit. Security by design thinking for developers and non developers.

Day 11: Real Case Studies (AI Security Failures)

Analyse real world incidents where AI systems were exploited. What went wrong, what should have been done, and what you would do differently.

Day 12: Hands On (Secure Your Own AI Tool)

Take an AI powered script from Week 2 and harden it. Apply everything from Days 9–11 in a guided lab exercise.

WEEK 4: Capstone, Portfolio & Career Readiness

Day 13: Capstone Project Kickoff

You will build a complete AI powered security tool — from threat detection to automated response. Brief, plan, and begin building.

Day 14: Capstone Build Session

Guided hands on build day. Elijah available for feedback and troubleshooting. Peer collaboration encouraged.

Day 15: Portfolio Building & Personal Branding

How to document your project for GitHub, LinkedIn, and CVs. Building a cybersecurity identity that gets you noticed.

Day 16: Demo Day + Cohort Graduation

Present your capstone project. Celebrate your progress. Transition into the 2 week internship briefing.
